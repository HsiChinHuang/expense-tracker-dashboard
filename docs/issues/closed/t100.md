## Goal

`backend/scripts/docs_verification.py` is the in-repo verifier whose output is
committed to `docs/criteria/docs_verification.md`. Its `INDEX_ANCHORS` table
still carries the same stale tests[133] digest that t99 just removed from the

the workflow-structure anchor and closes the generator-side half of the

stored leg 258 recover.

## Context (groom re-measurement, executed read-only against THIS checkout; the
survey numbers were re-measured, not inherited)

**The mechanism, from the source.** The anchor is checked inside
`check_index_and_stored_gates()` (`backend/scripts/docs_verification.py`), which
computes `entry_sha = sha256(json.dumps(entry, separators=(",", ":")))`, i.e.
canonical JSON with NO spaces, and compares `INDEX_ANCHORS[pos] == entry_sha`
(line 202). `INDEX_ANCHORS` is a `pos -> (issue_id, expected_sha256)` table with
**8 entries** (keys 16, 54, 131, 132, 133, 135, 215, 216), NOT 4 as survey
recorded. When an anchor mismatches the generator emits
`STORED_GATES FAIL tests[133] anchor identity/digest` -- that line goes to
**stderr only**; the report's only visible trace is the missing
`INDEX_ANCHORS PASS` line plus the `CHECKS_FAILED 1` footer. The survey
narrative line `anchor 133: FAIL digest mismatch (expected ..., got ...)` does
not exist in this generator and must not be reproduced by the builder.
