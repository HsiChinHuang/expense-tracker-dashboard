## Goal

Close the debt that has been disclosed on every merge since t90. The t90
amendment rewrote the stored command of tests[133] (the e2e/deploy workflow
structure gate), which changed that entry's canonical JSON and therefore its
`entry_sha`. Two OTHER stored legs - tests[190] and tests[191], both t47 - embed
the PRE-amendment digest of that entry as a byte-pin. They are still asserting
a digest the index no longer contains, so their byte-pin legs are a standing
false-failure source: they fire on a tree that has not drifted. This card
authors the coordinated amendment RECORD for those two legs so the orchestrator
can apply it in the strict merge window, on the t96 coordinated-set precedent
({232, 250} + the `entry_sha` coordination recipe).

This is the INDEX side of the anchor[133] debt. The GENERATOR side
(`backend/scripts/docs_verification.py`'s own `INDEX_ANCHORS[133]` constant) is
card t100 and is deliberately NOT this card's file.
